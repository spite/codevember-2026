The Orrery Collection

Every layout the machine can build, each in black enamel and gold. Channels run along great and small circles, a turntable sits at every crossing, and the faces between them become engraved plates. The engraving cycles through the five motif sets.

The layoutsThe finishes

Finishes

All twenty-two finishes, each on a different layout, with the bead sets and engraving motifs taking turns: polished metals, blued and two-tone pairings, verdigris and niello, and the champlevé enamels.

Rendered with the sketch's capture quality: PBR, soft shadows, GTAO and screen-space GI, AgX. Figures are counted from each layout as built: circles, turntables at their crossings, and plates between them, of which the ones with room carry a dial.
