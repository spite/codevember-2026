The Etcher's Pattern Book

Every blank, every pattern and every finish the sketch can etch, each rendered as it comes out of the process: scribed, hammered, bitten by the acid, patinated and burnished. The shapes are in copper with the Art Nouveau pattern; the patterns are on a silver sphere; the finishes each take a different shape and pattern.
